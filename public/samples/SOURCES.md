# Instrument samples

Both sets come from **tonejs-instruments** by Nicholaus Brosowsky
(https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments), samples licensed under
**CC BY 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

| Folder | Original recording | Notes kept |
|---|---|---|
| `guitar-acoustic/` | University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios (http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/) | E2 – C♯5, every minor third |
| `piano/` | Versilian Studios, VSCO 2 Community Edition (http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html) | A1 – D♯6, every minor third |

Files are unchanged copies of the originals; only a subset of the notes is kept; Tone.Sampler
pitch-shifts the notes in between. The site credits the source in its footer.
